What Can Be Reliably Made with AI in March 2026
Matching exhibit types to the right tools
Not all exhibits are created equal. Some a solo attorney can reliably generate with AI today. Others require domain expertise you don't have — and no amount of prompting fixes that. The difference comes down to whether YOU can verify the output.
High Reliability
Checklists, timelines, comparison charts — verifiable and defensible
Medium Reliability
Infographics, annotated photos, org charts — AI builds the layout, you verify every detail
Proceed with Caution
Scene reconstruction, medical visuals — requires domain expertise you don't have
