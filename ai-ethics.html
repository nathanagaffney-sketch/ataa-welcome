THE AI REGULATORY FRAMEWORK
The professional rules that govern AI use in legal practice
Competence
Rule 1.1
Lawyers must understand the technology they use and the risks associated with it.
Confidentiality
Rule 1.6
Lawyers must prevent confidential client information from being disclosed to AI systems.
Candor to the Tribunal
Rule 3.3
Lawyers remain responsible for the accuracy of all filings — including AI-generated content.
Supervision
Rules 5.1 & 5.3
Lawyers are responsible for supervising associates and staff using AI tools.
ABA Formal Opinion 512 discusses the application of these rules to generative AI.
